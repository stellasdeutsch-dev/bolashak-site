Uzbekistan

Universities of the Republic of Uzbekistan have allocated 20 educational grants for citizens of Kazakhstan

Under the Agreement on Cooperation in Higher and Postgraduate Education between the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the Government of the Republic of Uzbekistan dated 2 December 2022, universities of the Republic of Uzbekistan provide 20 educational grants for bachelor's degree programmes for the 2026–2027 academic year.

The grant covers the following expenses:

  • free tuition;
  • free accommodation in a dormitory;
  • free medical care, including polyclinic services;
  • free use of the library.

Please note!!! All other expenses, including transport, are covered by the grant recipient personally.

Language of instruction – Uzbek/Russian.

The full set of documents is accepted from 12 to 18 August 2026 (inclusive) through the “Electronic Government” portal (Egov.kz) https://egov.kz/ru/services/presale/higher_education_pass_203_mon or in person at JSC "Center for International Programs".

Applicants for study abroad are selected by passing the following rounds:

  1. A personal interview of applicants with members of the independent expert commission, including online.
  2. Review of the applicants' materials and a decision by the Final Commission.

The main criteria for selecting applicants are:

  1. level of foreign language proficiency on a points system;
  2. average grade of the education document on a points system;
  3. assessment by the Independent Expert Commission.

In accordance with paragraph 17 of the Rules, when selecting applicants for study abroad under international treaties/agreements, in the case of equal scores the following have priority (in this order):

  1. orphans and children left without parental care;
  2. persons with disabilities since childhood and children with disabilities who, according to a medical opinion, have no contraindications to study at the relevant foreign educational organisations, and who have submitted a medical certificate (for persons travelling abroad) in form No. 072/u, approved by order of the Acting Minister of Health of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 30 October 2020 No. QR DSM-175/2020 “On approval of the forms of accounting documentation in the field of healthcare” (registered in the Register of State Registration of Regulatory Legal Acts under No. 21579);
  3. children from large families;
  4. applicants from rural settlements who have lived in rural settlements for the last 2 (two) years;
  5. applicants who have an invitation from foreign higher and postgraduate education institutions, as well as those studying at such institutions.

Based on the results of the selection, the lists of recommended applicants will be sent to the host party, which will make the final decision.

Detailed information on submitting documents can be found in the guide.

In accordance with subparagraph 4) of paragraph 21, the winner of the competition is required to return to the Republic of Kazakhstan after completing academic studies abroad and to carry out continuous employment for a period of 1 (one) year.

Additional information is available by telephone: +7 (7172) 76-90-76 ext. 110, 112, 113, 114, 115, 301.

  • bachelor's degree – 3 places;
  • master's degree – 5 places;
  • doctoral studies – 2 places;
  • medical education (higher education) and residency – 2 places.

Bachelor's degree:

  • IELTS – at least 5.0;
  • TOEFL iBT – at least 40.

  • IELTS – at least 5.5;
  • TOEFL iBT – at least 50.

Each applicant may choose up to 3 higher education institutions and the corresponding educational programmes (specialities).
