Required documents

  1. ID card and passport;
  2. a state-issued document certifying general secondary education or technical and vocational education, or post-secondary education with an appendix;
  3. an electronic copy of diplomas of a first, second and third degrees of international Olympiads in general education subjects, international scientific project competitions and international performer competitions received over the last three (3) years, the list of which is determined by the Republican Commission, provided that the subject of the Olympiad or competition corresponds to the speciality chosen by them;;
  4. a valid official certificate of the established form on passing the kazakh language exam with the result corresponding to the established minimum requirements; - click to view Openadilet.zan.kz
  5. medical certificate in form 072/u for travelling abroad;
  6. a document confirming unconditional enrollment for academic studies (except for financial conditions) from a university that is included in the List of leading foreign universities. The document must specify: the applicant's data, specialty, terms of study, tuition fees. The document is provided with a notarized translation into the kazakh or Russian languages;
  7. a document confirming the applicant's enrolment for academic study on a free-of-charge basis, provided by a leading foreign higher education institution included in the list, as well as other foreign and/or Kazakh organisations, with full coverage of expenses, including payment of academic tuition for the entire period of academic study, with notarised translations into Kazakh or Russian, if available;;
  8. notarised consent of the legal representative (parents (parent), adoptive parents (adoptive parent), guardian or custodian, foster parent (foster parents), foster parents and other persons replacing them who, in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan, provide care, education, upbringing, protection of the rights and interests of the child) of the applicant for referral for study abroad (persons under the age of 18);
  9. motivation letter - an essay written in free form, not exceeding 500 words, in which the applicant justifies the chosen higher education institution and its advantages in this field, the need to receive this scholarship and the expected results of the knowledge gained in the chosen field and its further application in practice in kazakh or Russian languages. At the end of the motivational letter, the date and signature must be included

5 simple steps to submit documents

Step 1

Meet the requirements

Step 2

Collect a package of documents

Step 3

Submit documents within the specified time frame via the e-Government Portal

Step 4

Pass a competitive selection of 3 rounds:

Round 1 – Comprehensive testing;

Round 2 – Personal interview with IEC members;

Round 3 – meeting of the Republican Commission for Training Personnel Abroad

Step 5

After the award of the scholarship, the scholarship holder or his proxy must, within 90 calendar days from the date of the award of the scholarship, contact the Legal Service Department of the Center for International Programs JSC (hereinafter referred to as the Company) and conclude an agreement on the organization of training with the Company, as well as a guarantee agreement. In case of non-conclusion of contracts on time, the scholarship holder loses the status of the holder of the international scholarship "Bolashak".
