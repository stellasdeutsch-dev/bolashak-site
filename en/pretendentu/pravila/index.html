Dear Applicant

Below you can find a list of rules and requirements for submitting documents.

Applicants for a bachelor's degree
  • have an unconditional invitation (except for financial conditions) from a leading foreign university included in the List of recommended universities;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;
  • possession of a first, second, or third‑place diploma obtained in the past three (3) years in international Olympiads in general education subjects, international scientific project competitions, and international performance competitions, as determined by the Republican Commission, provided that the subject of the Olympiad or competition corresponds to the applicant’s chosen field of study.

  • List of International Olympiads in General Education Subjects, International Scientific Research Project Competitions, and International Competitions for Performers

Applicants for a master's degree
  • have an unconditional invitation (except for financial conditions) from a foreign university included in the List of recommended universities;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;.
Applicants for residency training
  • have an unconditional invitation (except for financial conditions) from a foreign university included in the List of recommended universities;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;.
Applicants from rural locality for obtaining a master's degree
  • Registration at the place of residence and labour activity in a rural settlement of the Republic of Kazakhstan for the last 2 (two) years;
  • the average score for the application of a bachelor's or specialist's degree must correspond to a score of at least 3.00 GPA (out of 4.00/4.33) or 4.00 out of 5.00;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;;
  • knowledge of a foreign language that meets the established minimum requirements; - click to view
  • correspondence of the specialty according to the bachelor's / specialist's diploma, the chosen specialty according to the list of related specialties
Engineering and technical workers for obtaining a master's degree
  • At the time of submission of documents, organisation and management of the production process (production, procurement, storage, transportation, processing/processing), higher technical education, including in the field of information and communication technologies, as well as direct implementation of production activities in the organisation, with a total experience in the chosen field of specialisation of at least 1 (one) year for training in technical specialties;
  • a bachelor's or specialist's degree specialty must correspond to the chosen specialty according to the list of related specialties;
  • the average score for the application of a bachelor's or specialist's degree must correspond to a score of at least 3.00 GPA (out of 4.00/4.33) or 4.00 out of 5.00;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;;
  • knowledge of a foreign language that meets the established minimum requirements. - click to view
Medical workers for obtaining a master's degree
  • At the time of submission of documents engaged in labor activity and providing direct medical care in medical organizations and having a higher medical education with a total medical experience of at least 1 (one) year;
  • a bachelor's or specialist's degree specialty must correspond to the chosen specialty according to the list of related specialties;
  • the average score for the application of a bachelor's or specialist's degree must correspond to a score of at least 3.00 GPA (out of 4.00/4.33) or 4.00 out of 5.00;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;;
  • knowledge of a foreign language that meets the established minimum requirements. - click to view
A civil servant for a master's degree
  • at the time of submission of documents for participation in the competitive selection, be a civil servant (except for political civil servants), with a total length of service in the civil service of at least 3 (three) years, including the last 12 (twelve) months in the sending state body;
  • a bachelor's or specialist's degree specialty must correspond to the chosen specialty according to the list of related specialties;
  • the average score for the application of a bachelor's or specialist's degree must correspond to a score of at least 3.00 GPA (out of 4.00/4.33) or 4.00 out of 5.00;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;;
  • knowledge of a foreign language that meets the established minimum requirements. - click to view
Employees of the nuclear industry for obtaining a master's degree
  • at the time of submission of documents, individuals employed by organizations involved in the state regulation of nuclear energy, subsoil use (uranium exploration and mining), the nuclear industry, or organizations engaged in engineering surveys, technical consulting, or scientific research in the field of nuclear energy; or organizations involved in the design, construction, and operation of infrastructure and core facilities; the development and implementation of promising technologies and production processes; the attraction of investment in the field of nuclear energy; or organizations engaged in the production of radiopharmaceuticals, radioactive isotopes and sources, the application of radiation technologies based on electron accelerators, and the development and use of nuclear physics analytical methods, with a total work experience of at least 3 (three) years, including actual work experience in the chosen field of specialization during the last 6 (six) months;
  • a bachelor's or specialist's degree specialty must correspond to the chosen specialty according to the list of related specialties;
  • the average score for the application of a bachelor's or specialist's degree must correspond to a score of at least 3.00 GPA (out of 4.00/4.33) or 4.00 out of 5.00;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;;
  • knowledge of a foreign language that meets the established minimum requirements. - click to view
Employees of the nuclear industry for obtaining a master's degree
  • have an unconditional invitation (except for financial conditions) from a foreign university included in the List of recommended universities;
  • at the time of submission of documents, individuals employed by organizations involved in the state regulation of nuclear energy, subsoil use (uranium exploration and mining), the nuclear industry, or organizations engaged in engineering surveys, technical consulting, or scientific research in the field of nuclear energy; or organizations involved in the design, construction, and operation of infrastructure and core facilities; the development and implementation of promising technologies and production processes; the attraction of investment in the field of nuclear energy; or organizations engaged in the production of radiopharmaceuticals, radioactive isotopes and sources, the application of radiation technologies based on electron accelerators, and the development and use of nuclear physics analytical methods, with a total work experience of at least 3 (three) years, including actual work experience in the chosen field of specialization during the last 6 (six) months; (only for employees of the nuclear industry).
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;

  • availability of an unconditional invitation (with the exception of financial conditions) from a foreign organization accepting an internship;
  • total work experience of not less than 3 (three) years, including the last 12 (twelve) months in the chosen field of specialization at the time of submission of documents continuously;
    For applicants in the categories “Users of artificial intelligence systems” and “Employees of the nuclear industry” the requirement is a total work experience of at least 3 (three) years, including actual work in the selected field of specialization for the last 6 consecutive months at the time of application;
  • the presence of a valid official certificate of passing the KAZTEST or Qazaq Resmi Test exam with a result of at least level B1;;
  • knowledge of a foreign language that meets the established minimum requirements. - click to view

Special Requirements for Certain Categories

For applicants falling under the category of “Users of artificial intelligence systems” - that is, individuals who directly use artificial intelligence systems in the course of their professional activities and/or in performing specific functions and tasks—there is a requirement to have at least 6 months of actual continuous work experience in their chosen field of specialization as of the date of submission of documents.

The category “Employees of the nuclear industry” includes individuals engaged in work at government regulatory agencies in the fields of nuclear energy use and subsoil use (uranium exploration and mining), the nuclear industry, engineering surveys, technical consulting, and scientific research in the field of nuclear energy, as well as in organizations engaged in the design, construction, and operation of infrastructure and core facilities; the development and implementation of promising technologies and production processes; the attraction of investment in the field of nuclear energy; the production of radiopharmaceuticals, radioactive isotopes and sources, the application of radiation technologies based on electron accelerators, and the development and use of nuclear physics analytical methods. For this category of applicants, the requirements include a minimum of 3 years of total work experience and at least 6 months of actual work experience in the chosen field of specialization as of the date of application submission

Rules of participation

Rules of participation

The competitive selection consists of 3 (three) rounds.

First round of competitive selection – comprehensive testing

At this stage, the applicants will pass:

  • tests for the analysis of verbal and numerical information aimed at determining the level of ability to analyze new information in a short time;
  • a personal questionnaire that allows you to structurally collect information about the applicant's personal and business qualities and the applicant's psychological readiness to study abroad. The focus is on the potential for the manifestation of competencies that allow effective communication and adaptation in a new environment, successfully coping with difficulties emotionally, making decisions and implementing plans.
Sample questions:
  1. Numerical test - click to view
  2. Verbal test - click to view
  3. Personality questionnaire - click to view

Before passing the test, you will have the opportunity to answer sample questions without taking into account time.

It is recommended to start comprehensive testing by passing numerical or verbal tests, as they require more focus and data analysis.

You can use a standard calculator, pen and paper. Please note that it is prohibited to use the calculator on smartphones, gadgets and other communication devices. Repeated testing is not provided.

IMPORTANT!

  • Comprehensive testing serves as a qualifying round for advancement to the next stage. Candidates who fail to meet the minimum threshold set by the governing body in the first round are not eligible to proceed to the second round.
  • Comprehensive testing is accompanied by audio and video recording.
  • Before the test, ensure that your computer is functioning properly, you have a stable internet connection, and your camera and microphone are working correctly.
  • If the browser window (site) is closed, the results of the selection stage are automatically canceled.
  • If the site tabs are closed, the results of the selection stage are automatically canceled, the site tabs must be open.
  • If you start taking one of the test types, you must complete the test to the end. Otherwise, the results of the selection stage are automatically canceled.

The second round of the competitive selection process is an anonymous personal interview with members of the Independent Expert Commission (IEC).

The second round is organized and conducted by the administrator in collaboration with members of the Independent expert commission (IEC). During this stage, the candidate’s level of professional training, theoretical knowledge, and professional competencies in the chosen field of specialization are assessed. Based on the information provided in the candidate’s motivation letter, IEC members provide recommendations for each applicant.

What awaits you on the day of the Interview?

You can prepare for the questions of this block. The training will allow you to present your knowledge, skills, abilities and abilities to the IEC members profitably.

It is IMPORTANT for candidates applying under preferential categories to also prepare for questions regarding their intended study program, foreign university, and country of study.

Demonstrating your own study of the proposed training program during the interview will show that you really thoroughly approach the issue of your education.

Collect as much objective information as possible about the country of study, the university where you plan to enroll or have enrolled. The official websites of foreign universities provide all information on the training program, on the requirements for candidates, and may also contain information about the scientific and teaching staff of the selected faculty / school. It is recommended to familiarize yourself with international rankings of universities by academic, subject or regional areas.

By your answers to the questions you should convince the NEC that you are worthy to become the owner of the international scholarship «Bolashak».

The third round of the competitive selection process is a meeting of the Republican Commission for Training Personnel Abroad.

The Executive Body submits candidates' materials to the third round for review by the Republican Commission for Training Personnel Abroad. This review includes the results of comprehensive testing, recommendations from the Independent Expert Commission, and ranking in accordance with the officially approved selection ranking table.

In the third round, the Republican Commission reviews the results of the first and second rounds and makes the final decision on awarding or denying the «Bolashak» scholarship. Candidates are notified of the decision through the publication of information on the administrator’s official website after the meeting protocol is signed.

5 SIMPLE STEPS TO SUBMIT DOCUMENTS

Step 1

Meet the requirements

Step 2

Collect a package of documents

Step 3

Submit documents within the specified time frame via the e-Government Portal

Step 4

Pass a competitive selection of 3 rounds:

Round 1 – Comprehensive testing;

Round 2 – Personal interview with IEC members;

Round 3 – meeting of the Republican Commission for Training Personnel Abroad

Step 5

After the award of the scholarship, the scholarship holder or his proxy must, within 90 calendar days from the date of the award of the scholarship, contact the Legal Service Department of the Center for International Programs JSC (hereinafter referred to as the Company) and conclude an agreement on the organization of training with the Company, as well as a guarantee agreement. In case of non-conclusion of contracts on time, the scholarship holder loses the status of the holder of the international scholarship "Bolashak".
